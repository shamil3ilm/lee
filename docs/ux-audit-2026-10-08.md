# lee: expert UX audit (Oct 8, 2026)

Auditor's brief: a senior product-design review of **lee** at `main` (8f89761, AI Radar merged), done the way a B2B/SaaS design lead would review a dashboard product before a design-system push. Everything below was observed on the running app, not inferred from code, unless it says "(code)".

## 1. Method and evidence

| Item | What was done |
|---|---|
| Build under test | `next dev` + seeded PGlite + dev-only test sign-in (`tests/e2e/env.ts`), port 3190, offline AI fixtures and stubbed LaTeX/GitHub. |
| Static capture | `tests/e2e/visual/screens.spec.ts`: **68 authed targets + 3 public pages**, at 390 / 768 / 1280 px (mobile / tablet / desktop), light and dark, full-page. ## 2. Scores

### 2.1 Overall

**Overall: 6.1 / 10**: a functionally deep, unusually honest product on a solid token foundation, held back by **page-level information overload** and **navigation depth**. The pieces are good; the compositions are crowded.

| Dimension | Weight | Score | One-line justification |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Visual hierarchy and layout | 12% | **5.5** | Strong primitives, but key pages stack 3–4 banners and a filter card above the content (Discovery's first result sits at y≈740 of 900 on desktop and below the fold on tablet and phone). |
| Consistency (components, spacing, type, tokens, icons) | 10% | **6.5** | Tokens are exemplary (0 raw palette classes, 0 `dark:` colour overrides, no hex literals outside the documented exceptions). Components drift: three filter patterns, three toggle primitives, card titles at two sizes, native radios and file inputs, and page insets that differ (Radar). |

## 4. Findings

Priority: **P0** blocks or confuses users. **P1** clearly hurts. **P2** is polish. Effort: **S** < half a day, **M** 1–3 days, **L** a week or more.

## 8. What works (briefly)

- **Token discipline is excellent.** Zero raw palette classes, zero `dark:` colour overrides, and no hex literals in components outside the documented exceptions (code). That is why dark mode scores 8 with no extra effort.
- **No horizontal page overflow at any width**, in all 428 measured captures (`findings.jsonl`).
- **CLS ≈ 0 on every route**, and route skeletons on 17 segments.
- **Transparency is a genuine differentiator**: quoted evidence in "Why this score" and the comparison card, "Nothing is sent", per-field Public/Private, "never from AI output" on Radar, and consequence lists in destructive dialogs.
- **Prepare application and the problem workbench** are well-designed flows that need only copy polish.
- **EmptyState** is used widely and consistently, and has a next step.
